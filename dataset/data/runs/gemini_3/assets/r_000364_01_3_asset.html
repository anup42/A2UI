RYONG

FIRE AND HERBS

Ryong is a vegetarian restaurant with plenty of vegan options. We offer a modern Vietnamese cuisine, that is, homemade experimental streetfood with fresh spelt and pho noodles, unique bao-burgers with tempura crusts and bento boxes with a fine selection of vegetables, pickles and herbs.

Playing makes life better

Tennis boosts your fitness and builds lasting connections. No matter your age or ability, there’s a fun, safe way to enjoy tennis. Our programs are designed so you can play tennis your way.

New to tennis? Start with a program

It’s never been easier to get into tennis with specially designed coaching programs that get you or your child moving on the court. Our professional and verified coaches provide structured programs that work for beginners and have different levels for different ages and abilities.

Cardio Tennis

Discover Cardio Tennis, the ultimate group tennis workout for adults, set to music. It’s fun for all ages and abilities.

Hot Shots Tennis

Learn new skills, build confidence and make friends. It's tennis made fun and just for kids!

AO Holiday Programs presented by Weet-Bix™

The ultimate holiday program for kids. Active screen-free fun with tennis and games on and off the court.

There is tennis for everybody

Tennis Australia is committed to creating spaces where people of all backgrounds, identities and abilities feel welcome, safe and included on and off the court. From grassroots to elite levels, we’re working to make tennis a sport where everyone can play, lead and belong.

All Abilities tennis

All Abilities tennis is rethinking how the sport is played and who gets to play it.

LGBTQIA+ tennis

People of all sexualities, gender identities and expressions should feel safe, seen and supported.

First Nations tennis

Celebrating culture, connection and opportunity through tennis with community.

Multicultural tennis

A place where every culture and community can play, connect and belong.

And more ways to play than ever

Racquet sports like pickleball, padel and POP Tennis are building momentum around the world. The different racquets or paddles, court sizes and balls can make them easy to learn and quick to play with your friends – but there’s still a lot to learn and master.

Pickleball

Australia’s fastest growing racquet sport is perfect for a social hit and a good time. Everyone can pickleball!

POP Tennis

A twist on traditional tennis that plays quick and social. It’s a small court game that’s big fun!

Padel

The global phenomenon is here and available at a growing number of locations across Australia.

Court hire

Hire a local court for a casual hit, practice session or match. There are more than 3000 tennis courts across Australia.

Looking to raise your game?

If you or your child are ready to take your game to the next level you can try 1:1 or group coaching. There are also 100s of competitions every year, so why not test your skills against other players in your local community.

Coaching for kids

Coaching gives kids more than just technique. It builds confidence, resilience and connection.

Coaching for adults

Whether you're looking to get fit, refine your skills or socialise, our qualified coaches can support you.

Compete as a kid

There are hundreds of tournaments played around Australia each year that cater for junior players of all levels.

Compete as an adult

Challenge yourself against other players of your level to improve your game and reach new levels of play.

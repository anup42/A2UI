Regeneron ISEF 2026 - Phoenix, Arizona

Regeneron ISEF is coming to Phoenix, Arizona! The Regeneron ISEF event week will be held from May 9-15, 2026 at the Phoenix Convention Center.

More than 500 volunteers will be needed across a spectrum of activities! Learn more about volunteering here and sign up to volunteer at Regeneron ISEF here!

More than 1000 judges are needed to determine the winners of the 2026 Regeneron ISEF! Learn more about serving as a Grand Award judge here and complete the judge application here!

Interested in competing? Use the links below to find an Affiliated Fair in your area, learn about the Updated 2026 International Rules, and find answers to Frequently Asked Questions.

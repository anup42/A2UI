Instantly recognisable by its dazzling lights and grand exterior, you’ll find our venue in the heart of thriving Victoria.

The Apollo Victoria Theatre is home to the West End’s hit musical, Wicked, which has been casting its spell over audiences across the world for two decades and is already the 9th longest running production in West End history. Book your tickets from the official site of the theatre.

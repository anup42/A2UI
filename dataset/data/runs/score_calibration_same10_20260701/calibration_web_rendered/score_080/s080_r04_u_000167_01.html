California Security-Deposit Return Checklist

Tenant checklist

For a California tenant who already moved out, the best approach is to organize proof of condition, cleaning, notice, keys returned, and timing before sending a polite demand letter.

In this scenario, the landlord sent the itemized deductions 24 days after move-out, which is later than California’s usual 21-day deadline for returning the deposit balance and itemized statement.

Demand Letter Outline You Can Use

Keep the tone calm, factual, and short. Ask for payment, not punishment.

Subject Line

Request for Return of Security Deposit Balance

Opening

State your name, rental address, and tenancy dates. State that the tenancy ended on 2026-02-28. State that you provided written notice 30 days earlier.

Short facts paragraph

Say you vacated the unit on 2026-02-28 and returned possession/keys. Say the apartment was professionally cleaned before move-out. Say you received the landlord’s itemized deduction statement 24 days after move-out.

Request paragraph

Request the prompt return of the deposit balance that you believe is owed. If some deductions appear unsupported or excessive, say you dispute those specific items and ask for supporting receipts, invoices, or explanation if not already provided. If the statement arrived after 21 days, politely note that California law generally requires the itemized statement and any remaining deposit to be sent within 21 days after vacating.

Documentation paragraph

Mention what you have available if needed: move-out photos/videos, cleaning receipt, copy of written notice, proof of key return, and copy of the itemized statement and envelope/email date.

Payment request

Ask that the amount owed be sent by a specific reasonable date, such as within 7 to 10 calendar days of the letter. Include where payment should be mailed or how it can be sent electronically if you want to offer that option.

Closing

Thank the landlord for resolving the matter promptly. Sign with your full name, forwarding address, email, and phone number.

Dashboard-Ready Business Insights

Insight 1: March is the strongest month so far

Revenue rose from 38,200 in January to 42,100 in March. That is a 10.2% increase over the 3-month period. Units increased from 755 to 817, so growth is not just price-driven.

Insight 2: Premium is growing faster than Standard

Premium revenue grew from 16,000 in January to 18,500 in March. That is +15.6%. Standard revenue grew from 22,200 to 23,600. That is +6.3%. This suggests Premium is becoming a stronger growth lever even though Standard remains larger overall.

Insight 3: South is closing the gap with North

North revenue was 20,000 in Jan, 21,000 in Feb, and 21,500 in Mar. South revenue was 18,200 in Jan, 19,200 in Feb, and 20,600 in Mar. The gap narrowed from 1,800 in January to 900 in March. South appears to be gaining momentum faster, especially in Premium.

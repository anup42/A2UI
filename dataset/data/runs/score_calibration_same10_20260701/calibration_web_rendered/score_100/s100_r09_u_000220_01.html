Family Picks for the Car

Option 1: But Why: A Podcast for Curious Kids

Kids send in real questions; thoughtful and fun

Best for ages around 7–12

Option 2: Wow in the World

Science-comedy style show with lots of kid appeal

Best high-energy podcast

Option 3: Circle Round

Folktales adapted for kids with strong storytelling

Best calm, story-rich segment

Option 4: The Wild Robot by Peter Brown

Adventure, heart, and excellent family discussion moments

Best audiobook for the first long stretch

Option 5: Charlotte's Web by E. B. White

Gentle, funny, and emotionally safe for a 10-year-old

Best comforting second audiobook

Option 6: Sing-Along Set

Mix of familiar, upbeat songs everyone can join

Best mood reset

Try 5–7 songs total, including: “Here Comes the Sun” – The Beatles; “Count on Me” – Bruno Mars; “Firework” – Katy Perry; “Yellow Submarine” – The Beatles; “Roar” – Katy Perry

Car Games That Work in Short Bursts

Road Trip Bingo

GameRoad Trip Bingo
How to playMake a simple checklist: red truck, bridge, cow, gas station, motorcycle, state plate. First person to get five wins.
Best forBest for: first hour and post-break resets.

20 Questions

Game20 Questions
How to playOne person thinks of a person, place, or thing. Everyone else gets 20 yes/no questions.
Best forBest for: medium-energy moments when you want group participation.

Alphabet Hunt

GameAlphabet Hunt
How to playFind letters A to Z on signs, license plates, and billboards. Play as a team so it stays cooperative.
Best forBest for: after a stop, when attention needs a visual task.

Would You Rather: Travel Edition

GameWould You Rather: Travel Edition
How to playAsk things like: “Would you rather ride in a camper van or a train sleeper car?” Keep it silly, not competitive.
Best forBest for: later hours when everyone wants conversation more than concentration.

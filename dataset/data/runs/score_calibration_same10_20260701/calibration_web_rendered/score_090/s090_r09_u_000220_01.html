Intergenerational Game Night Picks

Good fit for 18 guests ages 14 to 72

A good mix is a rotation of light, social, easy-to-explain activities with low rules overhead and positive themes. The options below are all learnable in under 10 minutes, require no hobby-gaming background, and avoid war themes or deception-centered play.

10 Easy-to-Learn Activities and Games

Just One

Activity/GameJust One
Ideal Group Size4–7 per table
Noise LevelLow–Medium
Why it works for mixed agesCooperative word-guessing keeps everyone involved; older and younger players can both contribute through vocabulary and shared knowledge.

Dixit

Activity/GameDixit
Ideal Group Size4–8 per table
Noise LevelLow
Why it works for mixed agesBeautiful image cards invite creativity without requiring speed, trivia, or strategy experience; good for different personalities and ages.

Telestrations

Activity/GameTelestrations
Ideal Group Size6–8 per table
Noise LevelMedium–High
Why it works for mixed agesDrawing plus guessing creates lots of laughter; artistic skill is not required, which helps level the field across generations.

Hues and Cues

Activity/GameHues and Cues
Ideal Group Size3–10 per table
Noise LevelLow–Medium
Why it works for mixed agesPlayers describe colors with simple words, so it is accessible, quick, and great for conversation without complex rules.

Ticket to Ride

Activity/GameTicket to Ride
Ideal Group Size2–5 per table
Noise LevelLow
Why it works for mixed agesRoute-building is intuitive and calm; turns are simple, and the theme is travel rather than conflict, which suits broad age appeal.

Qwirkle

Activity/GameQwirkle
Ideal Group Size2–4 per table
Noise LevelLow
Why it works for mixed agesPattern matching by color and shape is easy to teach and language-light, making it friendly for mixed ages and different experience levels.

Sushi Go Party!

Activity/GameSushi Go Party!
Ideal Group Size4–8 per table
Noise LevelMedium
Why it works for mixed agesCute art and short rounds make it approachable; choices are simple enough for beginners while still fun for adults.

Blank Slate

Activity/GameBlank Slate
Ideal Group Size3–8 per table
Noise LevelLow–Medium
Why it works for mixed agesMatching words encourages gentle humor and shared cultural references without putting pressure on quick reflexes or bluffing.

Bingo with themed prompts or prizes

Activity/GameBingo with themed prompts or prizes
Ideal Group Size10–30
Noise LevelMedium
Why it works for mixed agesFamiliar format, inclusive pacing, and easy hosting make it ideal for a larger community event with varied ages and comfort levels.

Team Charades or Picture Charades

Activity/GameTeam Charades or Picture Charades
Ideal Group Size8–20
Noise LevelMedium–High
Why it works for mixed agesNo materials-heavy setup, easy to adapt for mobility or comfort, and naturally creates cross-age teamwork and laughter.

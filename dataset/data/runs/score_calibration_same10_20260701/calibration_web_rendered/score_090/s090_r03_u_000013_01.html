Budget Trouble Areas

Dining out + groceries = $930. Food spending is high for one monthly budget, especially with both grocery and restaurant spending elevated.

Shopping at $380. This is one of the biggest discretionary leaks and can usually be cut quickly without affecting essentials.

Travel sinking fund at $300. Fine as a goal, but too aggressive while savings are below target.

Entertainment + subscriptions = $278. This is a meaningful drag for a budget currently saving almost nothing.

Housing is manageable but heavy. Rent is about 35.9% of net income, which is on the high side but still workable if lifestyle categories are tightened.

Practical Reallocation Plan

Keep fixed essentials mostly unchanged: rent, student loan, core utilities.

Reduce lifestyle spending first: dining out cap at $180, shopping cap at $150, entertainment cap at $100, and subscriptions trim to $30.

Bring groceries down modestly to $450 through meal planning and fewer top-up trips.

Cut travel sinking fund to $150 until savings habits are stable.

Send the freed-up cash directly to savings at the start of each month.

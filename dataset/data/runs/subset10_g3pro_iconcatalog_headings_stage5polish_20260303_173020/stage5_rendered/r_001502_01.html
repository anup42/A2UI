Scanning Environment and Permissions
This response assumes the user is operating a mobile device with a functional rear-facing camera. It is assumed that "linear barcodes" refer to standard retail symbologies such as UPC-A, UPC-E, or EAN-13 used for product identification. Camera permissions must be granted to the browser or host application to enable video capture for decoding.

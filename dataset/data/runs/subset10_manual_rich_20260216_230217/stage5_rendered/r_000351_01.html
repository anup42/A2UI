Decision Guidance for Walking Tours

Option 1: Hanoi | Best if you prefer cooler daytime walking conditions | Tradeoff: occasional mist/drizzle days

Option 2: Bangkok | Better if you want reliably warm conditions and nightlife comfort | Tradeoff: midday heat for long walking blocks

15-Minute Chicken Broccoli Pepper Stir-Fry (Serves 2)

Cooking Plan and Timing

  • Total active + cook time target: 15 minutes.
  • Protein: chicken breast, thin sliced for fast sear.
  • Sauce profile: savory soy, ginger, garlic with light gloss.

Ingredients Table

IngredientAmountNotes
Chicken breast300gSlice thin against grain
Broccoli florets180gSmall florets for fast cook
Bell peppers1.5 mediumMixed colors, thin strips
Garlic3 clovesMinced
Fresh ginger2 tspFinely grated
Soy sauce3 tbspBase savory
Water3 tbspSauce balance
Cornstarch1.5 tspThickening
Sesame oil1 tspFinish aroma
Neutral oil1.5 tbspHigh-heat stir-fry

Sauce Mix (Prepare First)

  • Whisk soy sauce + water + cornstarch + ginger + garlic until smooth.

15-Minute Step Sequence

MinuteAction
0-2Heat wok/pan to high, add neutral oil.
2-5Sear chicken until mostly opaque and lightly browned; remove.
5-8Stir-fry broccoli + peppers with 2 tbsp water, cover 60 sec.
8-11Return chicken, pour sauce, toss continuously until glossy.
11-13Reduce heat, finish with sesame oil, taste and adjust salt.
13-15Plate immediately over steamed rice or noodles.

Optional Flavor Tweaks

Option 1: Spicy Finish | Add 1 tsp chili crisp at minute 12 | Best for heat lovers

Option 2: Lower Sodium Variant | Use low-sodium soy and add 1 tsp rice vinegar | Cleaner profile

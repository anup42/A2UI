Kyoto 3-Day Temple and Tea Itinerary (Oct 15-17, 2025)

Trip Context and Budget Guardrails

  • Daily budget target: 15,000 JPY (food, transport, tickets; accommodation excluded).
  • Pace: relaxed, with longer temple blocks and tea-house stops instead of packed hopping.
  • Transport: mostly subway/JR plus short bus/taxi hops to avoid uphill fatigue.

Transport Sequence for Low Walking Load

  • Day 1: Kyoto Station -> Kiyomizu-Gojo (Keihan) + short taxi uphill.
  • Day 2: JR Kyoto -> Inari (JR Nara line), then Inari -> Tofukuji by local train.
  • Day 3: Kyoto -> Saga-Arashiyama (JR), then local taxi to return after sunset.

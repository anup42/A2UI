# Mysuru Heritage & Sightseeing Highlights

Mysuru offers a blend of historic royal architecture, sacred hillside shrines, and landscaped terrace gardens.

## Top 3 Attractions in Mysuru

### 1. Mysore Palace (Amba Vilas Palace)
Media: Image=https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Mysore_Palace_Morning.jpg Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/building.svg
A magnificent Indo-Saracenic royal residence renowned for its ornate durbar halls and thousands of lights that illuminate the facade on weekends and festive occasions.

### 2. Sri Chamundeshwari Temple
Media: Image=https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Chamundeshwari_Temple.jpg Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/geo-alt.svg
A revered hilltop temple perched atop Chamundi Hills, known for its towering seven-tiered Dravidian gopuram and panoramic views over the city.

### 3. Brindavan Gardens
Media: Image=https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Brindavan_Gardens,_Mysore.jpg Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/tree.svg
A sprawling terraced botanical garden situated below the Krishna Raja Sagara Dam, celebrated for its ornamental lawns and synchronized musical fountain shows.

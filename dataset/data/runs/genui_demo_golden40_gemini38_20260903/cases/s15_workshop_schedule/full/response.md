Here is a structured schedule for your 4-hour workshop (9:00 AM – 1:00 PM):

| Start Time | Duration | Session | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **9:00 AM** | 30 mins | **Welcome & Introductions** | Break the ice, set ground rules, review the day's agenda, and align on core objectives. |
| **9:30 AM** | 45 mins | **Brainstorming & Ideation** | Generate a wide pool of ideas, identify key challenges, and select top concepts to develop. |
| **10:15 AM** | 15 mins | **Coffee / Networking Break** | Rest, recharge, and allow informal discussion among participants. |
| **10:30 AM** | 75 mins | **Hands-on Prototyping** | Translate top ideas into tangible artifacts, mockups, wireframes, or actionable workflows. |
| **11:45 AM** | 50 mins | **Presentations & Feedback** | Each team/individual presents their prototype; gather constructive peer and facilitator feedback. |
| **12:35 PM** | 25 mins | **Wrap-up & Next Steps** | Summarize key takeaways, assign action items/ownership, and gather closing feedback. |

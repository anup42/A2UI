Here is your 12-item packing checklist for a three-day work trip, categorized for easy packing:

### **Documents**
1. **Government ID / Passport & Boarding Passes** – Essential identification along with physical or digital travel confirmations.
2. **Corporate Credit Card & Expense Receipts Folder** – For booking expenses, meals, and keeping physical receipts organized.
3. **Meeting Agenda & Business Cards** – Key notes, client itineraries, and business cards for networking.

### **Electronics**
4. **Work Laptop & Power Adapter** – Ensure it is fully charged before departure.
5. **Multi-Port Fast Charger & Phone Cables** – A compact dual USB-C/A charger to power both your phone and accessories.
6. **Noise-Canceling Earbuds / Headphones** – For taking calls on the go, focus, and travel transit.

### **Clothing**
7. **2–3 Professional Shirts / Tops** – Wrinkle-resistant button-downs or blouses suitable for client meetings.
8. **2 Pairs of Trousers / Slacks** – Versatile tailored pants that pair well across multiple shirts (one can be worn while traveling).
9. **Undergarments & Socks (4 pairs each)** – Pack enough for 3 full days plus one emergency spare set.

### **Toiletries**
10. **TSA-Approved Dental Kit** – Travel-sized toothbrush, toothpaste, and floss.
11. **Grooming / Skincare Essentials** – Travel-sized deodorant, facial moisturizer/sunscreen, and styling product or comb.
12. **Compact First-Aid & Medication Pouch** – Personal prescriptions, pain relievers, and band-aids for long days on your feet.

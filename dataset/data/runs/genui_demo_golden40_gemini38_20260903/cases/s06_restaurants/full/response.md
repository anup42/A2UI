# Top Indiranagar Dining Overview

Indiranagar is one of Bengaluru's prime culinary destinations, spanning street-level traditional eateries, bustling microbreweries, and intimate fine dining. These five standout restaurants highlight varied cuisines, price points, and dining atmospheres across the neighborhood's prominent 100 Feet Road and 12th Main food corridors.

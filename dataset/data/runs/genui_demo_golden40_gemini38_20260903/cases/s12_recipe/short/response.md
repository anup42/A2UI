**Ingredients (for 2):**
- 150g (3/4 cup) Arborio rice
- 200g mushrooms, sliced
- 1 small shallot or onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 60ml (1/4 cup) dry white wine
- 500–600ml warm vegetable or chicken broth
- 30g butter (divided)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 30g grated Parmesan cheese
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

**Steps:**
1. Sauté mushrooms in 1 tablespoon of butter over medium-high heat until browned; season with salt and pepper, then set aside.
2. Heat olive oil in the same pan, cook the shallots and garlic until translucent, add rice, and toast for 1–2 minutes until edges turn translucent.
3. Pour in the white wine, simmering until absorbed, then add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently until liquid is absorbed before adding more (about 18–20 minutes).
4. Remove from heat, fold in the cooked mushrooms, remaining butter, and Parmesan cheese, then cover for 2 minutes before serving.

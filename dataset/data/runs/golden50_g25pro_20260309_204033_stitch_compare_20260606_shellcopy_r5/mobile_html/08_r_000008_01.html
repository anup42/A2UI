Plan a two-adult Economy trip in May 2025: New York (JFK) to London (LHR), London to Rome (FCO), then Rome back to New York (JFK). Keep the search focused on Star Alliance carriers, one checked bag per passenger, and layovers under four hours.

Traveler Document Checks

Passport Validity: Both passports should be valid through at least late November 2025. Visa Rules: U.S. citizens generally do not need a short-stay tourist visa for the UK or Schengen Area, including Italy. ETIAS Status: Confirm the official ETIAS launch timing before purchase. Action: [Button: Check ETIAS Status] https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias_en

Booking Checks Before Purchase

Baggage: Confirm one checked bag per passenger is included on all three legs. Connections: Keep every layover under four hours and avoid airport changes in the same city. Names: Match first, middle, and last names exactly to both passports. Seats and Fare Rules: Check seat-selection fees, change fees, and cancellation terms before payment.

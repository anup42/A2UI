Your Personal Ethereal Beats Festival Itinerary

Here is a personalized schedule for your day at the Ethereal Beats Festival, designed to help you see your must-see artists: The Wandering Stars, Solar Flare, and Echo Bloom. The plan highlights a key time conflict you will need to navigate and provides options for your evening.

Planning Considerations

This itinerary assumes an average walking time of 10-15 minutes between stages. During peak hours with large crowds, travel time may be longer, so it's wise to start moving to your next destination a few minutes earlier than planned to secure a good spot.

The Main Event Conflict: 20:00 - 21:15

Your three priority artists have overlapping set times, creating a significant scheduling conflict. You will not be able to see the entirety of all three performances.

  • Solar Flare's set (19:00-20:30) overlaps with the start of The Wandering Stars (20:00).
  • Echo Bloom's entire set (20:15-21:15) runs concurrently with The Wandering Stars' performance (20:00-21:30).

You will need to leave Solar Flare's set early and then make a choice between seeing Echo Bloom or The Wandering Stars.

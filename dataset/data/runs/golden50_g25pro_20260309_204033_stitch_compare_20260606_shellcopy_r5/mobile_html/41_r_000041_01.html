Podcast Recommendations: Narrative-Driven Tech & Science

A Note on Your Listening Criteria This list is curated based on your preference for the structured, deep-dive storytelling found in 'Hardcore History' and 'Planet Money'. The recommendations focus on well-researched narratives and high production value, avoiding the unstructured conversational style you dislike. While episode lengths can vary, these podcasts generally align with your 25-40 minute target.

Cold Brew emerged as the clear leader in Q2, both in total sales and percentage growth, highlighting a strong market trend.

  • Top Performer (Q2): Cold Brew (1,850 units)
  • Highest Growth: Cold Brew (+105.6%)
  • Overall Trend: Cold drink sales surged by +91.4%, while hot drink sales declined by -16.7%.

Category Performance: Hot vs. Cold

Cold Drinks Surge

Media: Image=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a3/A_young_man_holding_a_glass_of_iced_coffee_at_home_at_sundown._Happy_hour_non-alcoholic_beverage.jpg/1280px-A_young_man_holding_a_glass_of_iced_coffee_at_home_at_sundown._Happy_hour_non-alcoholic_beverage.jpg Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/thermometer-sun.svg The cold drink category experienced explosive growth, more than compensating for the decline in hot beverages. This trend, led by Cold Brew and Iced Latte, indicates strong seasonal demand.

  • Total Q1 Sales: 1,750 units
  • Total Q2 Sales: 3,350 units
  • Total Change: +91.4%

Hot Drinks Decline

Media: Image=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/83/A_cup_of_cappuccino_at_Indooroopilly_Shopping_Centre.JPG/1280px-A_cup_of_cappuccino_at_Indooroopilly_Shopping_Centre.JPG Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/thermometer-snow.svg Sales for all hot drinks decreased from Q1 to Q2, with Hot Chocolate seeing the most significant drop. This suggests a typical shift away from warmer beverages as the weather changes.

  • Total Q1 Sales: 3,000 units
  • Total Q2 Sales: 2,500 units
  • Total Change: -16.7%

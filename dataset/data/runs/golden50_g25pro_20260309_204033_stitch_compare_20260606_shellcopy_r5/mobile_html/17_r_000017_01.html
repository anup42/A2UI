Classic No-Chill Chocolate Chip Cookies

This simple recipe delivers soft, chewy chocolate chip cookies straight from the bowl to the oven—no chilling required. Using a mix of brown and white sugar creates the perfect texture, with a rich flavor that's ready in under 30 minutes.

## Ingredients & Baking Guide

### Ingredients

Icon
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

### Baking Steps

Icon

1. Preheat & Prep: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two baking sheets with parchment paper. 2. Cream Butter & Sugars: In a large bowl, use an electric mixer to beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together until the mixture is light and fluffy, about 2-3 minutes. 3. Add Wet Ingredients: Beat in the egg and vanilla extract until just combined. 4. Combine Dry Ingredients: In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, baking soda, and salt. 5. Mix Dough: Gradually add the dry ingredient mixture to the wet ingredients, mixing on low speed until just combined. Be careful not to overmix. 6. Fold in Chips: Gently stir in the semi-sweet chocolate chips with a spatula. 7. Portion & Place: Drop rounded tablespoons of dough about 2 inches apart onto the prepared baking sheets. 8. Bake: Bake for 9-11 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden brown and the centers look slightly underdone. For chewier cookies, it's better to underbake slightly. 9. Cool: Let the cookies cool on the baking sheets for 5 minutes before transferring them to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Tips for Perfect Cookies

Icon
  • Room Temperature: Using softened (not melted) butter and a room temperature egg helps the dough come together perfectly for a uniform texture.
  • Don't Overmix: Mix the flour in until just combined. Overmixing develops gluten, which can make cookies tough instead of chewy.
  • Golden Edges: The key to a soft center is pulling the cookies from the oven when the edges are set and golden, but the middle still looks soft.
  • Storage: Store cooled cookies in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.

Which Phone Is Right For You?

Choose the iPhone 13 Pro if: Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/apple.svg

  • You prioritize video recording features like Cinematic Mode.
  • You want the absolute best-in-class raw performance for gaming and demanding apps.
  • You are already invested in the Apple ecosystem of devices and services.

Choose the Pixel 6 Pro if: Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/google.svg

  • You prioritize AI-powered still photography and features like Magic Eraser.
  • You prefer a pure, stock Android experience with timely updates directly from Google.
  • You value innovative software features like advanced speech recognition and live translation.

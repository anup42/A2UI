Kyoto Itinerary: A Day in Historic Higashiyama

Kyoto Itinerary: A Day in Historic Higashiyama

  • Date: Tuesday, November 5, 2024
  • Focus: A relaxed walking tour of Higashiyama's historical sites, starting from the Gion area. This plan assumes a comfortable pace with time for exploration and photography, all accessible on foot.

Higashiyama Walking Itinerary

Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/signpost-split.svg This itinerary guides you through the heart of one of Kyoto's most beautiful and well-preserved districts. All locations are connected by scenic walks, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in the historic atmosphere.

Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM): Kiyomizu-dera & Historic Lanes

Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/sunrise.svg

  • Begin your day at Kiyomizu-dera Temple, a stunning wooden temple famous for its large veranda offering panoramic views of the city. Arriving early helps avoid the largest crowds.
  • After exploring the temple, descend through the charming, stone-paved streets of Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka. These historic lanes are filled with traditional shops, cafes, and teahouses perfect for browsing.

Lunch (12:00 PM - 1:30 PM): Traditional Noodles

Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/cup-hot.svg

  • For lunch, enjoy a traditional meal at one of the many local eateries. A classic choice in this area is a comforting bowl of Soba (buckwheat) or Udon (wheat) noodles, served either hot in a savory broth or cold with a dipping sauce.

Afternoon (1:30 PM - 5:00 PM): Zen Gardens & Shrines

Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/sun.svg

  • Walk to Kodai-ji Temple, a beautiful Zen Buddhist temple known for its exquisite rock gardens and tranquil bamboo grove. It offers a more serene experience than Kiyomizu-dera.
  • Conclude your temple visits at Yasaka Shrine, located at the east end of Gion's main street. This vibrant and important shrine is always active and is beautifully illuminated in the evening.

Evening (From 5:00 PM): Exploring Gion

Media: Image=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/A_view_of_Gion_corner_in_Geisha_district%2C_Kyoto%2C_Japan.jpg/1280px-A_view_of_Gion_corner_in_Geisha_district%2C_Kyoto%2C_Japan.jpg Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/moon-stars.svg

  • As dusk falls, explore the world-famous Gion district. Take a walk down Hanami-koji Street, where you might catch a glimpse of a geiko (geisha) or maiko (apprentice geisha) on her way to an appointment.
  • For dinner, consider exploring the narrow, atmospheric Pontocho Alley, which runs parallel to the Kamo River and is lined with a diverse array of restaurants.

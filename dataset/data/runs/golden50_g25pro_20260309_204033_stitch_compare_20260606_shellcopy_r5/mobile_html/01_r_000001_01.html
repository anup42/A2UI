Rome's Ancient Wonders: A Family Guide

Here is a comparative guide to three essential historical sites in Rome, tailored to your family's trip in November. The plan considers your children's ages, interests, and budget, concluding with a recommendation for your first full day on November 12th.

Planning Notes for Your November Trip

This comparison assumes ticket prices for non-EU citizens booking online in advance, which is strongly recommended to secure entry times and avoid long queues. The costs are estimated for your family of four (2 adults, 2 minors under 18). The Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Palatine Hill are sold as a single 24-hour combination ticket. Your activity budget of €400 is sufficient for these main attractions, leaving a good amount for food experiences, other museums, or tours.

Recommendation for Your First Full Day: November 12th

For your first full day in Rome, I recommend visiting the Colosseum and the Roman Forum.

This combination is the perfect introduction to ancient Rome. Starting with these high-impact sites while the family is energetic will create a memorable first impression. They are conveniently located next to each other and are covered by a single ticket, making it a logistically simple and high-value choice. The experience directly aligns with your family's interest in history and will provide a fantastic foundation for the rest of your trip.

California Coast Photography Road Trip

California Coast Photography Road Trip

This 4-day itinerary guides you from San Francisco to Los Angeles along the scenic Highway 1, focusing on iconic coastal views and photography opportunities. The plan is paced to keep daily driving under 4 hours, allowing ample time for exploration and capturing the perfect shot.

Planning Note

Important: Sections of Highway 1, particularly in Big Sur, are prone to closures from landslides. Always check the latest road conditions before you depart.

Four-Day Driving Itinerary

Day 1: San Francisco to Carmel-by-the-Sea Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/signpost-split.svg

  • Route: San Francisco → Pacifica → Half Moon Bay → Carmel-by-the-Sea
  • Driving Time: Approx. 2.5 hours (125 miles)
  • Scenic Stop: Pigeon Point Lighthouse. This historic lighthouse offers classic coastal scenery and is a fantastic subject for photography, especially in the morning light.
  • Short Hike: Point Lobos State Natural Reserve. The Cypress Grove Trail (0.8-mile loop) provides stunning views of rugged coves, Monterey cypress trees, and potential sea otter sightings.
  • Overnight Stay: Carmel-by-the-Sea. A charming town with fairytale cottages, art galleries, and a beautiful white-sand beach perfect for sunset photos.

Day 2: Carmel to Cambria Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/camera.svg

  • Route: Carmel → Big Sur → Cambria
  • Driving Time: Approx. 3 hours (90 miles)
  • Scenic Stop: Bixby Creek Bridge. Pull over at the Castle Rock Viewpoint just north of the bridge for the quintessential photograph of this architectural marvel against the dramatic cliffs.
  • Short Hike: Partington Cove Trail. A moderately steep 1-mile round-trip hike down to a secluded, rocky cove accessed via a 60-foot wooden tunnel. It offers a different perspective on the Big Sur coast.
  • Overnight Stay: Cambria. A relaxing seaside village known for its art scene and proximity to Hearst Castle and the elephant seal rookery.

Day 3: Cambria to Santa Barbara Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/water.svg

  • Route: Cambria → Morro Bay → Pismo Beach → Santa Barbara
  • Driving Time: Approx. 2.5 hours (120 miles)
  • Scenic Stop: Morro Rock. This massive volcanic plug dominates the harbor. Photograph it from the Embarcadero or Morro Strand State Beach for beautiful reflections and compositions with local fishing boats.
  • Short Hike: Bluff Trail at Montaña de Oro State Park. This easy, flat 1.8-mile trail (one way) offers spectacular ocean bluffs, tide pools, and hidden beaches just south of Morro Bay.
  • Overnight Stay: Santa Barbara. Known as the "American Riviera," its Spanish-style architecture, palm-lined beaches, and the historic Stearns Wharf provide endless photo opportunities.

Day 4: Santa Barbara to Los Angeles Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/geo-alt.svg

  • Route: Santa Barbara → Malibu → Los Angeles (Santa Monica)
  • Driving Time: Approx. 2 hours (95 miles)
  • Scenic Stop: El Matador State Beach. Famous for its stunning sea stacks, arches, and hidden coves. A steep dirt trail leads to the beach. Visit during golden hour for breathtaking light.
  • Short Hike: Solstice Canyon Loop Trail. Located in the Santa Monica Mountains, this 1.9-mile trail leads you past coastal sage scrub to the ruins of a burned-down tropical-themed house and a seasonal waterfall.
  • End of Trip: Los Angeles. Conclude your coastal journey in the vibrant city of Los Angeles.

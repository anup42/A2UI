Eiffel Tower to Louvre: Travel Options

Eiffel Tower to Louvre: Travel Options

What This Comparison Assumes The following estimates are for one adult traveler during standard daytime hours. Travel times and costs can vary based on the time of day, traffic conditions, specific walking pace, and current public transport fares. Taxi prices are particularly sensitive to traffic.

Transportation Comparison Table

OptionApproximate TimeTypical Cost (€)ProCon
Walking45-60 minutes€0Very scenic route along the Seine River, passing many landmarks.Can be tiring; entirely dependent on weather conditions.
Metro25-35 minutes€2.15Fast and efficient, bypassing all street-level traffic.Can be very crowded, especially during peak hours; you miss the city views.
Taxi15-40 minutes€15 - €25Comfortable and direct door-to-door service, ideal for tired travelers.Most expensive option and can be significantly delayed by heavy traffic.

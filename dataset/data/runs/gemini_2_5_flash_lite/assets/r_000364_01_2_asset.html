CANCELLATION

Anything can happen, so we understand you might need to cancel your reservation. To keep you flexible and our business floating, we only charge no shows and late cancellations:

6 hours before: free cancellation
No show: 80 euros per person

ALLERGENS

We take the safety of our guests seriously and ask that you provide us with detailed information about any food allergies/intolerances in advance. Please be as specific as possible. Please note that we cannot offer a menu to people with the following food allergens/intolerances: celiac disease, fructose denial, histamine denial, cross-sensitivities, celeriac

ACCESSIBILITY

We gladly assist guests to our upstairs restaurant; however, we regret its limited accessibility for wheelchair users at this time.

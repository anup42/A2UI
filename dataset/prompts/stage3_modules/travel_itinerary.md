## Travel itinerary domain
- Use one compact Table with domain schedule, preferredPresentation cards, and primaryColumn dayDate/time/day.
- Preserve day/time order, area, route, morning/afternoon/evening activity, dining suggestion, travel time, scenic stop, overnight stay, and notes.
- Attach verified image/imageAlt only to the matching day/place row. If row-specific photos are missing, omit image columns; do not use icons or generic destination images as photos.
- Keep global travel guide/map/source actions near the itinerary section, not at the bottom as raw text.

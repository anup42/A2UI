## Recipe domain
- Render as title plus compact notes/hero card plus Ingredients and Instructions sections. Tabs are allowed for Ingredients/Instructions/Tips when it improves mobile readability.
- Ingredients should be compact Tables grouped by part when needed. Instructions should be step rows/cards with step number, title/action, and details.
- Preserve servings, prep time, cook time, dietary labels, temperatures, quantities, and timing exactly.
- Never emit unresolved placeholder strings like {{$item.title}}, {$item.title}, or ${$item.title}. Use real text or valid expression objects.
- Attach verified food/step images only near the hero or matching step; no detached gallery.

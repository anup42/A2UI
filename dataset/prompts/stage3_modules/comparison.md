## Comparison domain
- Use compact Table data. Feature matrices are table-first with first column Feature/Metric. Entity comparisons are cards with primaryColumn Item/Product/Option/Model.
- Preserve pros/cons, recommendation, constraints, prices, scores, attributes, source links, and decision rationale.
- If verified media exists for compared entities, attach it through Table.props.entityMedia or row image fields. Do not create trailing media cards.
- Avoid empty card fields by choosing primaryColumn and highlightColumns that exist in every row.

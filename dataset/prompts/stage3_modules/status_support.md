## Status/support domain
- Use one compact Table with domain status and preferredPresentation cards for diagnostics, troubleshooting, incidents, service health, scanner/check-in/process flows, and state machines.
- Preserve step/state/component/status/action/expected/impact/notes fields.
- For Markdown Step 1/Step 2 sections, convert them into rows backed by state.diagnosticSteps or equivalent.
- Do not create decorative icon/image galleries. Inline icons are allowed only when they clarify status or severity.

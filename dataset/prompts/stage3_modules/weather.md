## Weather domain
- Render as a familiar weather UI: one title, a compact current/summary card, then a forecast/metrics Table.
- Use domain weather and preferredPresentation cards for forecast rows. Preserve condition, high/low/current temp, rain/precipitation, wind, humidity, UV, and clothing/travel recommendations when present.
- Weather or climate comparison tables should use domain comparison and cards when rows are cities/entities; keep temp, rain, sunshine, wind, humidity, and recommendation fields.
- Weather icons are Icon components only. Do not create destination or weather photo galleries.
- Keep source/action links such as live forecast near the weather section as Buttons.

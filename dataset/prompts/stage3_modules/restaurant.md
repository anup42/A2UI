## Restaurant domain
- Treat restaurant lists as booking/place cards with domain booking unless a more specific renderer domain exists.
- Preserve cuisine, location/area, rating, price range, open/closed status, distance, family/veg/diet suitability, and reason.
- Row-level actions such as directions, reserve, call, menu, or source must stay in the row with actionLabel.
- Use verified restaurant/place images only when provided; otherwise omit images.

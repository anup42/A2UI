Itinerary

A Day in Historic Higashiyama, Kyoto

calendar_today Tuesday, November 5, 2024

Planning Notes

Your journey through Higashiyama begins in Gion. This route is designed to maximize the ethereal morning light and the quiet magic of Kyoto's most preserved district.

  • alarm Early start recommended to avoid crowds at the temple gates.
  • forest Autumn foliage peaks around this time; expect vivid crimson maples.
  • steps Stone slopes and stairs ahead. Wear comfortable walking shoes.

09:00 AM

Morning Ascent

Kiyomizu-dera Temple

Experience the panoramic wooden stage that juts out over the hillside. The view of Kyoto city framed by seasonal colors is unparalleled.

11:00 AM

Historic Streets Walk

Wander through Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka. These stone-paved slopes are lined with traditional shops and teahouses.

View Walking Map

12:30 PM

Traditional Lunch

Pause for Yudofu (tofu hot pot), handmade Soba, or Udon in a quiet courtyard setting.

02:00 PM

Kodai-ji Temple

Famous for its exquisite Zen rock gardens and bamboo grove. A more serene alternative to the busier temples.

04:00 PM

Yasaka Shrine

The vibrant vermilion gateway at the edge of Gion. Beautiful as the lanterns begin to glow at dusk.

06:00 PM

Gion Evening Stroll

Explore Hanamikoji Lane. Keep an eye out for geiko and maiko moving between appointments in this atmospheric district.

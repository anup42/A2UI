The Sun-Seeker's Choice

While both capitals share pleasant daytime highs, Lisbon emerges as the archival favorite for October. With warmer evenings (+3°C compared to Rome) and an extra hour of daily sunshine, it offers a more consistent Mediterranean warmth for late-season travelers.

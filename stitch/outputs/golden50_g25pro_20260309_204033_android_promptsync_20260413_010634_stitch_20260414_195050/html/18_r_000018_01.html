Hearty Lentil Shepherd's Pie with Cauliflower Mash

Savory lentil and mushroom filling topped with creamy, dairy-free cauliflower mash. A comforting staple reimagined for mindful eating.

Total Time 80 mins
Servings 6
Difficulty Medium
lightbulb

Chef’s Notes

  • • Use brown or green lentils for the best texture; red lentils will become too mushy.
  • • Best baked in a 9x13 inch ceramic dish.
  • • Ensure all broth and spices are certified GF for strict diets.

Ingredients

Cauliflower Topping

  • 1 large cauliflowerchopped
  • 1/2 cup coconut milkfull fat
  • 1/4 cup nutritional yeast
  • 2 cloves garlicminced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & Pepperto taste

Lentil Filling

  • 1 cup dry lentilsrinsed
  • 8 oz mushroomsdiced
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 carrotsfinely diced
  • 1 large onion
  • Tomato paste2 tbsp
  • Thyme, Rosemary, Paprika
  • Coconut aminos1 tbsp
  • Frozen peas1/2 cup

Method

01

Prepare Cauliflower Topping

Steam or boil cauliflower florets until tender (approx 10-12 mins). Drain thoroughly and place in a food processor with coconut milk, nutritional yeast, garlic, and oil. Blend until silky smooth and creamy.

02

Preheat & Sauté

Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). In a large skillet or pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Sauté diced onion and carrots until softened. Add mushrooms and garlic, cooking until mushrooms have released their moisture and browned.

"The secret is the coconut aminos—it provides that deep umami base that traditionally comes from beef broth."
03

Cook Lentils & Finish Filling

Stir in tomato paste, herbs, and rinsed lentils. Pour in vegetable broth and coconut aminos. Simmer for 20-25 minutes until lentils are tender. Stir in frozen peas and a cornstarch slurry to thicken the gravy slightly.

04

Assemble & Bake

Transfer the lentil mixture to your baking dish. Spread the cauliflower mash evenly over the top, using a fork to create decorative peaks. Bake for 20-25 minutes until the topping is lightly golden around the edges.

05

Rest & Serve

Let the pie rest for 10 minutes after removing from the oven. This allows the filling to set slightly, making it much easier to scoop and serve beautifully.

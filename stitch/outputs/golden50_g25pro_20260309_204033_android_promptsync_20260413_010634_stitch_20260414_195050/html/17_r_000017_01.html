Classic No-Chill Soft Chocolate Chip Cookies

The ultimate recipe for those who crave immediate gratification. These cookies boast an exceptionally soft, pillowy texture that melts away, yet they require zero chilling time. Perfect for beginner bakers who want a professional result without the wait.

Yields 2 dozen
Bake Time
9-11 mins

Recipe Notes for Perfect Texture

  • • Ensure your butter is softened but not greasy. It should yield to gentle pressure.
  • • Room temperature eggs emulsify better into the dough, creating that signature soft crumb.

The Process

01

Prepare Oven

Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.

02

Cream & Mix

Cream together softened butter and sugars until light and fluffy. Beat in eggs and vanilla until fully incorporated.

03

Dry Ingredients

Mix in flour, baking soda, and salt. Stir in chocolate chips by hand. The dough should be thick and heavy.

04

Shape & Bake

Roll into 1-inch balls and place on prepared sheets. Bake for 9-11 minutes until edges are just barely golden.

05

Cool

Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to set completely.

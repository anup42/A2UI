Kyoto: 3-Day
Family Discovery

High-impact historical sites, manageable outdoor activities, and engaging food experiences curated for children ages 8 and 10. Experiencing the peak cherry blossom season (April 5-7).

01
auto_stories Southern Higashiyama

Fushimi Inari & Kiyomizu-dera: Iconic History

Start at Fushimi Inari Taisha. Hike to Yotsutsuji intersection (45-min round trip). Afternoon: Kiyomizu-dera for views and Ninenzaka for kid-friendly shops.

Fushimi Inari Guide arrow_outward
02
forest Arashiyama District

Nature & Hiking: Bamboo Grove & Monkey Park

Saga-Arashiyama Station. Bamboo Grove early to avoid crowds. Cross Togetsukyo Bridge to Iwatayama Monkey Park (20-min hike). Lunch: local udon and matcha soft-serve.

Monkey Park Info arrow_outward
03
restaurant North & Central

Food & Culture: Kinkaku-ji & Nishiki Market

Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) path is visually striking. Explore Nijo Castle for its famous "nightingale floors." End at Nishiki Market for skewers, tamagoyaki, and traditional sweets.

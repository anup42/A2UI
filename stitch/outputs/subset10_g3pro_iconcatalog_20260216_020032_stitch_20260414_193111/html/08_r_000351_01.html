Bangkok vs Hanoi in January

The Curator’s Summary

January offers vastly different experiences. Bangkok is cool and dry with sunny, warm afternoons. Hanoi is in winter—cooler, overcast, and wrapped in a misty drizzle. Hanoi is more comfortable for walking, but less photogenic than the vibrant, sun-drenched capital of Thailand.

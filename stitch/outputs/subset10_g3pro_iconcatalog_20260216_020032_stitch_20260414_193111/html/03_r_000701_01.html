Vegan Mushroom Risotto

Master the art of mantecatura—a traditional Italian technique to achieve silkiness without dairy, using the natural starches of Arborio rice and an emulsified fat finish.

timer
Prep Time 45 Mins
person
Servings 4 Persons

Ingredients

The Cooking Phases

01

The Sauté & Toast

Sauté shallots and mushrooms until golden. Add the rice and toast until the edges are translucent, essential for flavor depth.

02

The Slow Simmer

Add warm broth one ladle at a time. The agitation releases starches, creating the legendary creaminess.

03

The Mantecatura

The final finish. Stir in vegan butter or nutritional yeast vigorously to emulsify the remaining liquid into a sauce.

Plating & Serving

Garnish with fresh parsley, a drizzle of truffle oil, and cracked black pepper. Serve on warmed plates immediately.

Vegan Mushroom Risotto

Master the art of the dairy-free 'mantecatura'. This technique uses starchy rice water and umami-rich mushroom broth to achieve a luxurious creaminess that rivals any traditional butter-heavy version.

timer
Prep Time 45 mins
groups
Servings 4 People

Ingredients

Cooking Phases

1

The Sauté & Toast

Flavor Foundation
thermostat Medium High

Sauté mushrooms until golden and moisture has evaporated. Remove half, then add shallots and rice. Toast the rice until edges are translucent.

play_circle Watch Toasting Technique
2

The Slow Simmer

Patience & Agitation
thermostat Medium

Deglaze with wine. Add warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring constantly to release starches. Wait for liquid to absorb before adding more.

3

The Mantecatura

Emulsification
thermostat Off Heat

The final emulsification. Stir in nutritional yeast or vegan butter vigorously. The risotto should flow like lava (all'onda).

info What is Mantecatura?
4

Plating & Serving

Visual Excellence

Plate immediately. Top with the reserved crispy mushrooms, fresh thyme, and a finishing drizzle of cold-pressed olive oil.

Bangkok & Hanoi

Atmospheric Divergence

January offers a stark sensory contrast between these two hubs. Bangkok basks in its dry prime, characterized by golden sunlight and warmth. Conversely, Hanoi embraces a poetic winter—cool, misty, and draped in a perpetual grey that turns walking tours into moody explorations of colonial history.

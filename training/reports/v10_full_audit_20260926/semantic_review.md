# v10 source-to-target semantic review

Audit date: 2026-09-26. Dataset: `training/outputs/datasets/full_data_archive_recovered_v10/{train,val}.jsonl`. This review did not change a dataset record, generate replacement IR, train a model, use the network, or render on a device.

## Scope and judgment boundary

I streamed all **91,115 train + 1,862 validation = 92,977** current pairs through [semantic_scan.py](semantic_scan.py) to retrieve candidate semantic gaps. The scanner checks short bullets, option fields, Markdown cells, source/reference labels, number strings, malformed source table widths, and explicit chart specifications. These are **candidate counts, not confirmed defect counts**: spelling, paraphrase, numeric formatting, hidden state and alternative visual organization can trigger them.

Separately, I manually read the complete source and target for a fresh deterministic sample of **18 train + 2 validation** pairs. [semantic_fresh_sample.py](semantic_fresh_sample.py) uses reservoir sampling with train seed `2026092601` and validation seed `2026092602`, excluding the exact 90 train and 10 validation IDs in the earlier manual-100 package. Eligible populations were **91,025 train** and **1,852 validation**. All 20 sampled IDs and source-family IDs are distinct from the earlier 100. The full current pairs and row SHA-256s are in [semantic_fresh20.jsonl](semantic_fresh20.jsonl); manually authored judgments are in [semantic_fresh20_verdicts.json](semantic_fresh20_verdicts.json).

Fresh-20 verdicts: **16 KEEP, 3 REPAIR, 1 REJECT/REGENERATE** (train: 14/3/1; validation: 2/0/0). KEEP means no material issue found by this bounded source-to-target review. It does not certify external product, salary, medical, legal or live-link claims. The sample is too small, and validation was oversampled, to extrapolate these fractions to the full corpus. Additional targeted cases below were retrieved by flags and have no sampling interpretation.

## Training implications and next steps

The examples expose distinct supervision risks: a model can learn to omit short instructions and entire sections, mislabel table fields while preserving raw words, replace requested charts with tables, erase attribution names, or repeat incorrect arithmetic from a flawed source. Source-answer errors need source verification/correction followed by target regeneration. Target-only errors need prompt/pipeline/renderer fixes and Stage 3 regeneration. Per `AGENTS.md`, do **not** hand-edit an IR label to make a sample pass. Hold these pairs out until a documented regeneration and fresh review; do not delete the original evidence.

The existing v10 semantic gate's `source_units()` skips lines containing `|` and prose shorter than 45 characters/eight words, so it cannot reliably catch lost pipe-separated options or concise steps. Its chart condition specifically looks for a `line chart`, not every explicit chart type. Citation checks focus on reference-token visibility; hidden-state checks focus on `alert`/`date` fields. These limits explain the confirmed failures despite valid Express syntax. Full scanner or manual results here do **not** establish a model-training failure rate or prove causation for any deployed model's behavior. Original user questions are absent from the earlier manual-100 sample, so response-to-UI fidelity also does not establish original request relevance.

GenUICraft includes ARM64 native runtime components derived from these projects:

* LiteRT 2.2.0, Copyright Google LLC, Apache License 2.0.
  Source artifact: com.google.ai.edge.litert:litert:2.2.0
  https://dl.google.com/dl/android/maven2/com/google/ai/edge/litert/litert/2.2.0/litert-2.2.0.aar
* LiteRT-LM v0.16.1, Copyright Google LLC, Apache License 2.0.
  https://github.com/google-ai-edge/LiteRT-LM/tree/v0.16.1/prebuilt/android_arm64

The LiteRT-LM Top-K OpenCL sampler is modified at packaging time. Its existing
ELF DT_SONAME entry is changed to DT_NEEDED and its string is changed from
"libLiteRtTopKOpenClSampler.so" to "libLiteRt.so". No code, section offsets,
Android packed relocations, or hash tables are changed. The reproducible script
is tools/prepare_gemma4_gpu_runtime.py.

Full upstream, output, and license hashes are recorded in ON_DEVICE_RUNTIME.md
and enforced by the preparation script. The upstream licenses and the LiteRT
third-party notice are packaged beside this file.

## What initialization can and cannot be separated into

`Gemma4Runtime.initializeEngine()` validates the corrected model, loads GPU libraries, checks MTP capability, prepares a PID-scoped GPU shader-cache directory, and then constructs and initializes the native `Engine` (`genuicraft/src/main/java/com/samsung/genuicraft/sdk/provider/Gemma4Runtime.kt:260-325`). The corrected-model validator reads its manifest and SHA-256-hashes the 2.68 GB model once per file identity per process (`GpuFp16Correction.kt:39-88`). These operations are inside the request's engine-initialization wall timer (`Gemma4Runtime.kt:98-105`). There are **no individual timer markers** around validation, capability lookup, cache-directory setup, native weight loading, or shader compilation. A log gap is therefore an elapsed window, not a measured duration for any one of those activities.

## Decode evidence and limits

The **exact logged graph** is a `decode` signature with 2072 ops, 35 inputs, and 32 outputs, fully placed in one OpenCL delegate partition; `verify` and `mtp_drafter` are separate GPU-delegated graphs (current raw `:22915-22918,22967,23449,23634-23641`). A delegate partition is a graph grouping, **not one OpenCL kernel**. `delegate_kernel.cc` names the wrapper source file and does not identify an executing GEMV/matmul/attention kernel. The corrected-FP16 adapter later reports Q/DQ source/patch counts with zero rejected programs (filtered `:557,:560`); those counts establish patch installation, not where runtime is spent.

For the two completed SM-F776U BXP-003 baseline requests, 3394 input and 428 output tokens each were saved. Native decode rates are 67.30 and 71.06 tokens/s, giving derived decode durations of 6.36 and 6.02 s; native prefill rates give derived 2.38 and 1.66 s (`per_request.csv`, rows for `R3GL203AKSF/baseline_cold_mtp_on`). Thus decode is the larger **estimated generation phase** for this prompt after initialization, but not the largest cold-request phase in every row. The 08:45:33.894/40.255 log warnings say prefill/decode *profile summaries* could not be obtained (`logcat_raw.txt:23971,24343`); high-level token/rate metrics are still present in `per_request.csv`. There are no successful per-op/per-kernel durations or GPU memory/compute counters here. Consequently, the particular decode kernel, effective GPU bandwidth, bandwidth-bound versus compute-bound status, decoder FLOP count, and achieved decoder GFLOPS **remain unresolved**. Token/s and graph placement alone do not establish any of those.

The older Fold8 SDK-demo log separately shows the same four main signatures and a GPU-delegated `mtp_drafter` at 01:06:17.712–33.185 (`../20261011_new_grpo_e2b_fold8/diagnostics/20261011_010654/logcat_filtered.txt:1674-1886`). It corroborates graph topology only. Its elapsed initialization intervals and later 01:08 MTP acceptance event belong to that older sample, not the current inference profile.

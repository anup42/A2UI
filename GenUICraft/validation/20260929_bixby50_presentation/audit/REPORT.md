# Bixby50 presentation and recovery audit

This is a saved-artifact review dated 29 September 2026. I read all 50 frozen source responses and their 50 corresponding archived raw outputs, checked each archived raw SHA-256 against the original per-case manifest, and verified each archived source text against the frozen corpus. I also read all 27 completed rank-64 GPU FP32 raw outputs and their matching frozen source text. No model call, device replay, app edit, or new benchmark was made for this audit.

[Case inventory](case_inventory.json) records paths, hashes, raw status, saved repair strategy, and exact compiled A2UI reachability/text counts for every case. [Machine-readable findings](findings.json) holds the prioritized examples and direct evidence paths.

The two absent screenshots are BXP-002 capture timeouts. Both compiled documents pass the renderer contract; the saved evidence does not prove a blank screen. The partial run stopped at the user's request. MTP-on BXP-013–015 and both modes BXP-016–050 are missing. The full50 and partial results belong to different model/runtime configurations and should never be pooled into a current-model 50-case score.

The most recurrent material defects are:

1. **Generated content can be invisible.** In the FP32 compiled graphs, 6/15 MTP-off and 4/12 MTP-on cases contain at least one meaningful Text node disconnected from `root`. BXP-002 is raw-valid in both modes but reaches only 5/10 components and 4/7 Text nodes: PM2.5, citations, and all outdoor guidance are orphaned. BXP-005 reaches only 9/28 off and 9/27 on components, hiding ticket/pass and interchange guidance; off also repeats operating hours. BXP-004 hides a baggage price caveat in both modes and a premium fare exception on. These are graph-link candidates only when the source fact already exists in the generated output.
2. **Other facts are absent or changed in generation.** BXP-001 on is raw-valid and fully reachable, yet converts 57%/55%/55% rainfall values to unitless 57/55/55. BXP-009 on reconnects generated Vaishali Nagar content after repair, but never generated a C-Scheme downside. BXP-012 off generated the free-tier/export uncertainty caveat but orphaned it; on never generated it. Edge repair cannot recover absent values or missing uncertainty.
3. **Recovery can create a structurally sound but poor answer.** In the archived full50, 48 generated-only repairs rendered, but all 48 failed source integrity; 46 lacked citation markers and 37 lacked or changed numeric facts. Only one retained a complete generated graph. BXP-029 salvage shows ₹50 lakh as ₹500 lakh and ₹5,000 as ₹500000; BXP-032 changes 10% to 0%; BXP-047 changes battery figures and loses table relationships. These are unsafe to accept automatically as factual conversions.
4. **Presentation order and schema labels matter.** FP32 BXP-013 off repair reaches 19/19 components and 16/16 Text nodes, but its screenshot starts with a table ahead of the “One-year FD rates” title. The source's descriptive headers become `bank/rate/size/date`. BXP-008 repairs restaurant rows but shortens “Approx. cost per person” to `cost`. BXP-006 off repair displays generated grammar/catalog/profile hashes as user-facing prose. BXP-007 both modes repeat a step and hide the closing comparison.

Citation marker presence is a limited diagnostic: 9/15 off and 8/12 on compiled FP32 graphs are missing at least one marker from the source, but the count does not check whether a surviving marker is attached to the right claim. Reachability is compiled structure, not pixel-level visibility or a full screenshot audit. The historical Perplexity answers are frozen source material; this audit does not validate their factual truth or currency.

The strongest regression targets are BXP-002 and BXP-005 for disconnected generated detail, BXP-001 for unit loss despite full reachability, BXP-009 and BXP-012 for distinguishing orphaned from never-generated caveats, BXP-013 for table order/labels after repair, and BXP-029/BXP-032/BXP-047 for rejecting corrupted numbers. BXP-010, BXP-011, and BXP-015 are useful positive controls with broadly preserved source facts in the inspected FP32 graph.

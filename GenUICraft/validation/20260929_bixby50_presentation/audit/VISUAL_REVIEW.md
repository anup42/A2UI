# Saved Bixby50 device replay visual review

This review compares saved-output `express_repair_only` replays on the connected Fold7. The before replay used SDK 0.5.5; the final after replay used the SDK 0.5.6 build with the BXP-018 chart fix. Both replay the same historical Express files with `modelCalls: 0`, `inferenceEvaluated: false`, and no source-text fallback. Raw Express bytes were verified unchanged across all 77 saved cases. The rank-64 GPU FP32 files remain a partial 15 off / 12 on corpus, not a fresh or complete current-model benchmark. The archived full50 files came from the earlier original E2B v10 W4 configuration and must not be combined into a rank-64 score. The linked `device/after` captures come from the final installed app; the earlier candidate capture is preserved separately under `device/intermediate_before_chart_fix`.

The FP32-off replay rendered 15/15 before and after; FP32-on rendered 12/12 before and after. The after runs used generated DSL repair in 9/15 off and 8/12 on, compared with 3/15 and 4/12 before, because structurally valid but disconnected content is now repaired. Source-integrity acceptance did not rise: 3/15 off and 1/12 on in both runs. Renderability and structural recovery therefore do not establish source-faithful answers.

The archived full50 final after replay rendered 48/50 saved generated outputs. BXP-009 and BXP-039 were rejected as malformed/gibberish and never rendered; there were no render failures or historical coverage warnings. This recovers a visible partial display for BXP-013 and BXP-027, which were blank in the before replay. All 48 rendered cases still failed source-integrity acceptance. Initial screenshots were reviewed for all 48 in the first capture, and final BXP-013/018/027 and the rank-64 positive controls were checked after the chart fix; horizontal and vertical captures were checked for the listed long cases. Thus 48/50 is a renderability result, not a measure of complete or faithful answers.

Across the historical set, the largest recurring presentation issue is wide tables with multiple clipped columns in the first viewport (for example BXP-002/004/007/014/015/016/019/022/025/031/032/035/041/045/048). Several outputs are much too sparse for their source, producing one line and a large empty screen (BXP-001/008/013/026/038/040/042). Corrupted digits, missing units, and merged words are recurrent generated-content defects. BXP-018 demonstrated a distinct renderer loss of values that were present in a recovered Table; the final build fixes that loss without changing the generated values. Structural repair can make existing fields reachable, but cannot validate or reconstruct facts never generated.

All screenshots are at one device/font configuration. Visual review does not establish factual currency of the frozen Perplexity answers or every off-screen item. The final replay retained 15/15 off, 12/12 on, and 48/50 historical render coverage; the two historical rejections are unchanged. The device result confirms presentation recovery and the BXP-018 fix, not acceptance of the historical answers as source-faithful.

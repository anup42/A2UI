GRE Quant Study Plan: 5 Weeks to Test Day

GRE Quant Study Plan: 5 Weeks to Test Day

Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/journal-text.svg

This 5-week study plan is structured to systematically cover all major GRE Quantitative Reasoning topics. It allocates 90 minutes for study on weekdays and 3 hours on Saturdays, leading up to your test on August 30, 2024. Each week builds on the last, culminating in full-length practice tests.

Study Plan Context

Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/calendar-check.svg

  • Test Date: Friday, August 30, 2024
  • Plan Start Date: Monday, July 29, 2024
  • Weekly Schedule: 90 mins/day (Mon-Fri), 3 hours (Sat), Sunday is for rest.
  • Structure: Each week focuses on a core topic area, ending with a focused practice quiz. The final week is dedicated to full-length test simulation and review.

Weekly GRE Quant Study Schedule

This schedule provides a detailed breakdown of your study sessions, focusing on one major topic area per week before a final review.

WeekDatesFocus AreaKey Sessions (90 min/day)Saturday Goal (3 hours)
1Jul 29 - Aug 3ArithmeticMon: Number Properties; Tue: Fractions & Ratios; Wed: Percentages; Thu: Exponents & Roots; Fri: Word ProblemsReview week's topics (1 hr); Take Arithmetic quiz (1 hr); Deeply review all incorrect answers (1 hr).
2Aug 5 - Aug 10AlgebraMon: Equations & Inequalities; Tue: Functions; Wed: Quadratic Equations; Thu: Systems of Equations; Fri: Algebraic Word ProblemsReview Algebra concepts (1 hr); Take Algebra-focused quiz (1 hr); Review incorrect answers, noting patterns (1 hr).
3Aug 12 - Aug 17GeometryMon: Lines, Angles & Polygons; Tue: Triangles; Wed: Circles; Thu: 3D Solids; Fri: Coordinate GeometryReview formulas & concepts (1 hr); Take Geometry-focused quiz (1 hr); Review all solutions, even correct ones (1 hr).
4Aug 19 - Aug 24Data AnalysisMon: Statistics (Mean, Median, SD); Tue: Probability; Wed: Combinatorics; Thu: Data Interpretation (Charts/Graphs); Fri: Mixed Problem SetsReview Data Analysis (1 hr); Take a comprehensive quiz on all topics from Weeks 1-4 (1.5 hrs); Quick review of mistakes (0.5 hr).
5Aug 26 - Aug 29Final ReviewMon: Full-Length Practice Test 1; Tue: In-depth review of Test 1 results; Wed: Full-Length Practice Test 2; Thu: Final review of weak spots & formulasTest Day is Friday, Aug 30. Get a good night's sleep. Have a light breakfast and avoid last-minute cramming.

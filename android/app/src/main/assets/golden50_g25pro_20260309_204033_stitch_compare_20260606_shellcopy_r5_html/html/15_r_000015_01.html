Project Plan: New Company Blog Launch

This plan outlines the key phases, tasks, and timeline for launching a new company blog, starting on August 1, 2024, with a target launch date of October 15, 2024.

Planning Context

This project plan assumes a small, dedicated team consisting of a project lead, content writer(s), a designer, and a developer. It also presumes the use of a standard Content Management System (CMS) like WordPress, Ghost, or Webflow, and that core company branding guidelines are already established. The timeline includes buffers for feedback and iteration.

Timeline and Task Breakdown

The project is divided into five distinct phases, with some activities running in parallel to ensure an efficient workflow.

Detailed Task List by Phase

Phase 1: Planning & Strategy (Due by 2024-08-14)

  • Define blog objectives and key performance indicators (KPIs).
  • Identify and document the target audience and reader personas.
  • Conduct a competitive analysis of 3-5 competitor blogs.
  • Establish 3-4 core content pillars or categories.
  • Brainstorm and finalize the first 15-20 article topics.
  • Select and confirm the CMS platform.
  • Finalize and get stakeholder approval on the Content Strategy document.

Phase 2: Design & Branding (Due by 2024-08-30)

  • Create low-fidelity wireframes for the blog homepage, article page, and category/archive pages.
  • Develop high-fidelity mockups incorporating company branding.
  • Design templates for featured images and social sharing graphics.
  • Present designs to stakeholders for feedback and revisions.
  • Obtain final sign-off on all design elements.

Phase 3: Development & Technical Setup (Due by 2024-09-20)

  • Set up the hosting environment and install the chosen CMS.
  • Develop the blog theme based on the approved designs.
  • Ensure the site is fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
  • Install and configure essential plugins for SEO, analytics, caching, and security.
  • Integrate the blog with an email marketing platform for newsletter subscriptions.
  • Set up user roles and permissions for writers and editors.
  • Conduct initial developer QA and bug fixing.

Phase 4: Content Creation (Due by 2024-10-04)

  • Assign and draft the 5 required launch articles.
  • Source or create all necessary images, videos, or graphics for the articles.
  • Complete copyediting and proofreading for all launch content.
  • Upload and format the final 5 articles into the CMS staging environment.
  • Optimize each article for on-page SEO (titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, internal links).
  • Prepare a backlog of at least 3-5 additional articles for post-launch publication.

Phase 5: Pre-Launch & Launch (Due by 2024-10-15)

  • Conduct comprehensive end-to-end testing on the staging site (check all links, forms, and social sharing functionality).
  • Set up Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and any other tracking tools.
  • Create a detailed launch promotion plan (e.g., email announcement, social media campaign, internal communication).
  • Perform a final content and design review with all stakeholders.
  • Deploy the blog to the live server on the target launch date.
  • Execute the launch promotion plan.
  • Monitor site performance, analytics, and user feedback for the first 48 hours.

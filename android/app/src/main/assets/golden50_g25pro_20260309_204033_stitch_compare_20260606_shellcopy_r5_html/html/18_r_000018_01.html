Hearty Vegan & Gluten-Free Shepherd's Pie

Hearty Vegan & Gluten-Free Shepherd's Pie

Media: Image=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/ff/Granville_Island_Market_-_Shepherds_pie.jpg/1280px-Granville_Island_Market_-_Shepherds_pie.jpg

This comforting shepherd's pie is completely vegan, gluten-free, soy-free, and nut-free. It features a rich, savory lentil and mushroom filling topped with a creamy mashed cauliflower crust. Perfect for a wholesome family meal that is ready in under 90 minutes.

Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/list-task.svg

  • Serves: 6 people
  • Prep Time: 25 minutes
  • Cook Time: 55 minutes
  • Dietary: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Soy-Free, Nut-Free

Recipe Ingredients

For the Savory Lentil Filling:

  • Olive Oil: 2 tbsp (30 ml)
  • Yellow Onion: 1 large, finely chopped (approx. 220g)
  • Carrots: 2 medium, finely diced (approx. 150g)
  • Celery Stalks: 2, finely diced (approx. 100g)
  • Cremini Mushrooms: 8 oz (225g), chopped
  • Garlic: 3 cloves, minced (approx. 15g)
  • Brown or Green Lentils: 1 cup (200g), rinsed
  • Vegetable Broth: 2 cups (480 ml), ensure it's gluten-free
  • Tomato Paste: 2 tbsp (30g)
  • Coconut Aminos: 2 tbsp (30 ml), for a soy-free umami flavor
  • Dried Thyme: 1 tsp (2g)
  • Dried Rosemary: 1 tsp (2g)
  • Cornstarch: 1 tbsp (10g) mixed with 2 tbsp cold water
  • Frozen Peas: 1 cup (150g)
  • Salt and Black Pepper: to taste

For the Creamy Cauliflower Topping:

  • Cauliflower: 1 large head, cut into florets (approx. 2 lbs / 900g)
  • Nutritional Yeast: ¼ cup (15g)
  • Unsweetened Oat or Rice Milk: ¼ cup (60 ml)
  • Soy-Free Vegan Butter: 2 tbsp (28g)
  • Garlic Powder: ½ tsp (2g)
  • Salt and White Pepper: to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Prepare Cauliflower & Oven: Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Place the cauliflower florets in a large pot, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Cook for 10-15 minutes, or until fork-tender.

2. Sauté Aromatics: While the cauliflower cooks, heat the olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the chopped onion, carrots, and celery. Sauté for 6-8 minutes until softened.

3. Cook Mushrooms: Add the chopped mushrooms and cook for another 5-7 minutes, until they have released their moisture and started to brown. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for one more minute until fragrant.

4. Build the Filling: Stir in the tomato paste, dried thyme, and dried rosemary. Add the rinsed lentils, vegetable broth, and coconut aminos. Bring the mixture to a simmer.

5. Simmer & Thicken: Reduce the heat to low, cover, and let the filling simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the lentils are tender. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and frozen peas. Cook for 2-3 minutes more until the gravy has thickened. Season with salt and pepper to your liking.

6. Make the Cauliflower Mash: Drain the cooked cauliflower very well. Return it to the pot and add the soy-free vegan butter, oat/rice milk, nutritional yeast, and garlic powder. Use an immersion blender or a standard blender to puree until smooth and creamy. Season with salt and white pepper.

7. Assemble the Pie: If your skillet is not oven-safe, transfer the lentil filling to a 9x9 inch (23x23 cm) baking dish. Spread the mashed cauliflower evenly over the top of the filling. Use a fork to create decorative lines or peaks on the surface.

8. Bake to Perfection: Place the shepherd's pie on a baking sheet to catch any potential drips. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the filling is bubbly and the cauliflower topping is lightly golden brown. You can switch to the broiler for the last 1-2 minutes for extra browning, watching carefully to prevent burning.

9. Rest and Serve: Let the pie rest for at least 10 minutes before serving. This allows the filling to set and makes it easier to serve. Enjoy

Handmade Candle Business Launch Plan

Handmade Candle Business Launch Plan

Media: Image=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/15/Handmade_Pillar_Candles_01.jpeg/1280px-Handmade_Pillar_Candles_01.jpeg

This 3-month plan outlines the key phases and tasks to launch your handmade candle business, starting September 1, 2024. The goal is to be ready for your first sale by November 30, 2024, building on the materials and designs you have already prepared.

3-Month Launch Roadmap: September - November 2024

Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/calendar-check.svg This timeline breaks down your launch into manageable monthly goals, from legal setup to your first sale.

Phase 1: Foundation & Legal (September 2024)

  • Goal: Establish the business entity, finalize product presentation, and begin content creation.
  • Key Tasks: Register your business name and obtain any necessary local permits; Design and order all packaging (boxes, labels, warning stickers); Conduct quality control test batches for consistency and safety; Set up business social media accounts (e.g., Instagram, Pinterest); Begin product photography and videography.
  • Deliverable: A legally registered business entity; Finalized and ordered packaging; A set of quality-tested products; Active social media profiles with initial content.

Phase 2: E-commerce & Marketing Setup (October 2024)

  • Goal: Build the online store and create a backlog of marketing content.
  • Key Tasks: Select and set up your e-commerce platform; Design the online storefront to reflect your brand; Write compelling product descriptions for all 10 candle designs; Create complete product listings with high-quality photos; Determine your pricing and shipping strategy.
  • Deliverable: A fully functional online store; Completed product listings with pricing; Defined shipping rules and costs.

Phase 3: Launch & Operations (November 2024)

  • Goal: Go live with the store, drive initial traffic, and efficiently manage the first orders.
  • Key Tasks: Conduct final tests of the online store and checkout process; Announce the official launch date and build excitement on social media; Officially launch the store at the beginning of the month; Consider running a small launch promotion (e.g., free shipping on first orders); Fulfill and ship all initial orders promptly.
  • Deliverable: A publicly launched store; First sales processed and fulfilled; A working system for order management.

Choosing Your Online Store Platform

Selecting the right platform is a critical early decision. Here are two excellent options for handmade businesses.

Option 1: Shopify | A powerful, scalable, and customizable e-commerce solution.

  • Best for: Building a standalone brand and having full control over your store's design, features, and customer data.
  • Consideration: Requires a monthly subscription fee and you will be responsible for driving all of your own traffic.

Action: [Button: Explore Shopify] https://www.shopify.com/

Option 2: Etsy | A popular marketplace with a large, built-in audience searching for handmade goods. Media: Icon=https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/bootstrap-icons@1.11.3/icons/shop.svg

  • Best for: Reaching an existing customer base quickly with minimal setup and marketing effort.
  • Consideration: You operate within Etsy's framework, facing more direct competition and paying listing and transaction fees.

Action: [Button: Get Started on Etsy] https://www.etsy.com/sell

Rome vs Lisbon: October Climate Pick

Rome vs Lisbon: October Climate Pick Media: Image=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/ba/Drawing%2C_St._Peter%27s_Basilica_Seen_from_the_North_against_the_Hills_and_Skyline_of_Rome%2C_January_1869_%28CH_18195387%29.jpg/1280px-Drawing%2C_St._Peter%27s_Basilica_Seen_from_the_North_against_the_Hills_and_Skyline_of_Rome%2C_January_1869_%28CH_18195387%29.jpg Media: Image=https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e2/Cloudflare_and_EY_Office_Complex_%E2%80%93_Modern_Architecture_in_Alc%C3%A2ntara%2C_Lisbon_%2854708346011%29.jpg/1280px-Cloudflare_and_EY_Office_Complex_%E2%80%93_Modern_Architecture_in_Alc%C3%A2ntara%2C_Lisbon_%2854708346011%29.jpg

Lisbon is the better October choice for a tourist who wants warmer evenings and more sunshine. Both cities average the same 22 C daytime high, but Lisbon is warmer at night and gets about one more sunshine hour per day.

Recommendation

Choose Lisbon if your priority is a warmer, sunnier October trip. Rome is still mild and has one fewer rainy day on average, but Lisbon's warmer evenings and extra sunshine make it more comfortable for late walks, outdoor dining, and sightseeing.

Climate Average Caveat

These are long-term climate averages, not a live forecast. Check a local forecast close to the travel date before packing.
